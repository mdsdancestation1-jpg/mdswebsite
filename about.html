The MDS Evolution

Founded in 2013, M.D.S DANCE STATION began as a humble vision to provide world-class dance education in an environment that nurtures both the artist and the athlete.

We believe that dance is a universal language of kinetic energy. From our first studio session to becoming a premier institution, our mission remains unchanged: to empower individuals through the discipline and beauty of movement.

THE VISIONARY

Minku Kumar

Founder & Head Choreographer

With over 20 years of professional experience on national and international stages, Minku Kumar brings a unique blend of technical precision and soulful expression to the studio.

His philosophy centers on the belief that "everyone is a dancer; some just haven't found their rhythm yet." His mentorship has helped hundreds of students transition from hobbyists to professional performers.
