Crafting Motion for Over a Decade

Founded by the visionary choreographer Minku Kumar, M.D.S DANCE STATION has been the heartbeat of the local dance community for over 12 years.

We believe dance is not just steps; it's a language of the heart. Our studio provides a professional environment for students of all ages to explore their creative potential through rigorous training and passionate expression.
